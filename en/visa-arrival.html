Student Card (I-Kad) Renewal

Starting from the second year, the I-Kad renewal costs only 50 RM annually.

Countries Requiring Additional Official Letters

Students from these countries need additional letters before obtaining the visa:

  • 🇸🇩 Sudan → NOC Letter
  • 🇴🇲 Oman → NOL Letter
  • 🇱🇾 Libya → LOE Letter
  • 🇮🇷 Iran → LOE Letter

Airport Arrival Procedures

Important Instructions Before Travel
  • You must inform the university of your arrival date at least 3 to 7 days before your flight.
  • Avoid arriving in Malaysia on public holidays to prevent any delays in airport clearance procedures.
  • Elmnoor provides airport pickup services to assist you with clearance and safely transport you to your accommodation.
