Unexpected Fines and Fees

Beware of these fines if you pay late

Lincoln University

If the full first-year fees are not paid before your arrival in Malaysia, a fine of 500 RM will be imposed immediately upon arrival.

City University

Any outstanding balance not paid on time will incur a late fine of 100 RM per month.

UTM Space

After receiving the "Initial Procedures Letter", you will have to pay an additional 700 RM as a fee to issue the final Offer Letter.

Visa Fees at NUMed

The EMGS visa processing fee is non-refundable. However, if you do not use the Visa Approval Letter (VAL), you can get a refund for the visa security bond (2,000 RM) after submitting a formal written refund request to the university.
