5 Steps to Register in Malaysian Universities

The correct sequence to complete your registration smoothly

  1. Prepare Academic Documents

    Essential documents required by all Malaysian universities:

    • Full Passport — A copy of all pages
    • Academic Transcripts — All names must match the passport
    • Registration Form — Usually filled out online
    • Personal Photo — With very strict conditions (see below)
    Personal Photo Conditions — Rejected by Malaysian Immigration if it doesn't meet:
    • Pure white background with no shadows on the face or background
    • Head perfectly centered with no tilting
    • Strictly prohibited: Glasses (even prescription) + Jewelry + Hair covering any part of the face

  2. Provide Proof of English Proficiency

    Each university has its own requirements. The table below outlines the minimum requirements:

    University IELTS TOEFL Important Note
    NUMed (Newcastle) 7.0 100 Or Band 4 in Malaysian MUET
    Nottingham (Foundation) 6.0 — —
    Nottingham (Bachelor/Postgraduate) 6.5 — —
    UTM (ELS Institute) — — Must reach Level 108 to start Bachelor's
    City University — — Language courses available (~2,000 RM per level)
    Lincoln (Nursing) 6.0+ — IELTS only — No exceptions

  3. Pay Application Fees

    Application fees vary greatly between universities. These fees are mostly non-refundable:

    University Application Fee Note
    Monash Malaysia 106 RM Lowest among universities
    Nottingham Malaysia 424 RM —
    UTM (Main Campus) 450 RM —
    UTM Space 700 RM Paid after receiving the initial procedure letter
    City University 1,000 RM Equivalent to ~$250 USD

  4. Receive Offer Letter

    After submitting the documents, the university reviews your application and issues the initial Offer Letter. Some universities might require a fee payment to confirm admission before issuing it.

    Alert: UTM Space University

    After receiving the "initial procedure letter", you must pay an additional 700 RM to extract the final Offer Letter.

    Refund Policy — Nottingham University

    If you withdraw and do not use the Visa Approval Letter (VAL), the EMGS fees are non-refundable. However, the visa insurance (2,000 RM) can be refunded upon submitting a formal written request.

  5. Obtain Visa (EMGS) and Medical Examination

    EMGS fees include: Visa Processing + Medical Examination + Health Insurance + Student Card (I-Kad).

    University Total EMGS Fees Visa Duration
    UTM (Bachelor's) 2,800 — 3,200 RM Depends on program length
    UTM (Foundation) Included in the 13,100 RM One Year
    Nottingham (Foundation) 3,150 RM One Year
    Nottingham (Bachelor's) 3,900 RM 3 Years
    Lincoln University 3,500 RM —
    Countries Requiring Additional Formal Letters (NOC/NOL/LOE)
    • 🇸🇩 Sudan → NOC Letter
    • 🇴🇲 Oman → NOL Letter
    • 🇱🇾 Libya → LOE Letter
    • 🇮🇷 Iran → LOE Letter
    Important Alert Upon Airport Arrival

    Inform the university of your arrival time 3 to 7 days in advance. Avoid arriving during Malaysian public holidays to prevent clearance delays. Elm w Noor provides an airport pickup service.

    Renewing the Student Card (I-Kad)

    In the second and third years: renewing I-Kad costs only 50 RM annually (in the case of a 3-year visa like Nottingham's).

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to the most common questions from students and parents

When do registration periods open for Malaysian universities?

Registration intakes vary from one university to another:

  • City University: January, May, September — and July for Nursing only
  • Lincoln University: March, June, September, November
  • UTM (Phase 1): Apply April 1 — May 15 (Offer letters on June 9)
  • UTM (Phase 2): Apply June 23 — July 17 (Offer letters from July 27)
  • UTM (Foundation Program): Registration closes on April 30
How much is the cost of living for an Arab student in Malaysia per month?

Malaysia is one of the most affordable study destinations in Asia. You can live comfortably with $400-700 per month:

  • On-campus housing (UTM Johor): 395 — 760 RM per month
  • On-campus housing (UTM KL): 385 — 935 RM for a single room. Apartment: 1,800 RM
  • Off-campus housing: 250 — 800 RM per month
  • Lincoln Area (Petaling Jaya): Relatively higher due to its proximity to the capital
  • Food: $10-15 daily (Full meals)
  • Transportation: $30-50 per month (Metro and Buses)

The UNITEN campus in Putrajaya is considered an excellent choice — a safe "green corridor" with nearby malls and hospitals.

Can I study Medicine in Malaysia? And what are the requirements?

Yes, Malaysia has excellent medical universities:

  • NUMed Newcastle (MBBS): 126,500 RM/year. Accredited by BMA and MMC. UKFP graduates get GMC after a year in the UK.
  • Lincoln University (MD): $15,000-17,200. Years 3-5 in Kota Bharu clinical campus.
  • UCSI University: Another great option.
Crucial Alert — Medical Internship

International students are not eligible for the House Officer internship in Malaysian government hospitals. You must plan for your internship in your home country or apply for the UKFP program in the UK (NUMed).

NUMed age requirements: 16 years for Foundation, 17 for Biomedical Sciences, 18 for the MBBS program.

Which majors require special conditions besides grades?
  • Electrical, Electronic, Mechatronics, Biomedical, and Chemical Engineering in UTM: Require passing a CBT computer test
  • Architecture in UTM: Personal interview + mandatory Portfolio
  • Industrial Design in UTM: Personal interview
  • Physiotherapy in UOC (Cyberjaya): Very limited seats — Early application is essential
How long is the Diploma program at UTM?

The Diploma duration at UTM varies by major:

  • 2 Years: Technology Management Diploma + Accounting Diploma
  • 2.5 Years: Computer Science + Urban Planning + Quantity Surveying + Property Management + Architecture + Sports Diploma
  • 3 Years: Land Surveying + Islamic Education + All Engineering majors (Civil, Electronic, Electrical, Mechanical, Chemical)
Can I transfer to study in the UK after Malaysia?

Yes, and this is one of the most prominent advantages of Nottingham Malaysia University:

  • (2+2) Engineering track: 2 years in Malaysia, then 2 years in the UK or China branch
  • Computer Science and AI: Transfer to the UK branch in the final year only
  • NUMed — (2+1) Biomedical Sciences track: 2 years in Malaysia + 1 year in Newcastle UK (10-week research project)
Why is Malaysia considered suitable for Muslim students?
  • Malaysia is an official Islamic country, ranked #13 globally in the Global Peace Index 2025
  • Food is 100% Halal in all restaurants and markets
  • Mosques are available everywhere and the call to prayer is 5 times daily
  • An Arab community exceeding 200,000 people
  • Arab student unions exist in all major universities
