أسعار جامعة ملتميديا (MMU)

Important Information about MMU Programs: Multimedia University offers comprehensive study programs in several specialized fields. Tuition fees below are indicative and may vary by semester and academic year. Fees do not include visa fees, health insurance, or accommodation. Fees can be paid in installments, and the university provides various financing options for international students.

Available Intakes: March/April, July/August, October/November

Basic Requirements: IGCSE minimum 5 credits in English and Mathematics, or A-Level minimum grade D, or equivalent from Arabic certificates with 50-60% or above.

Multimedia University (MMU)

Cyberjaya | Melaka

#851-900 World | #148 Asia

Malaysia's first private university, established in 1996 and managed by Telekom Malaysia. Known as a leader in technical and digital education with Premier Digital Tech Institution status. Home to a diverse student community of 13,000-15,000 students from over 60 countries. Specializes in IT, creative multimedia, animation, electrical and mechanical engineering, business and accounting, law, and strategic communication. Offers diverse study programs across two campuses: Cyberjaya (main campus in Malaysia's Silicon Valley) and Melaka (quiet academic environment with lower living costs).

Available Programs:

Bachelor's

  • • Computer Science & IT (AI, Cybersecurity, Data Science)
  • • Engineering - Cyberjaya: (Electronic, Electrical, Telecom) / Melaka: (Mechanical, Robotics)
  • • Creative Multimedia & Cinematic Arts - Cyberjaya only
  • • Business & Accounting
  • • Law - Melaka only
  • • Strategic Communication

Postgraduate

  • • Master's by Coursework
  • • Master's by Research
  • • Master's Mixed Mode
  • • PhD by Research

Foundation Year (Pre-University)

  • • Pre-university preparation program (1 year)
  • • Specializations: Sciences & Tech, Computing, Engineering, Multimedia, Business, Communication

MMU Highlights:

  • Strong industry partnerships: Collaborations with tech giants (Huawei, ZTE, Microsoft, Intel)
  • Two campuses: Cyberjaya (20-30 min from KL), Melaka (quiet academic environment)
  • Modern facilities: Smart labs, production studios, comprehensive sports facilities
  • High graduate employment rate with continuous training opportunities
  • Semester-based payment system with installment options within the semester
  • International partnerships with prestigious universities for exchange programs
  • Affordable on-campus and off-campus accommodation available

Rankings & Accreditations:

  • Malaysia's first private university with Premier Digital Tech Institution status
  • QS World University Rankings - Excellent in Engineering & Computing
  • High graduate employment rate - Preferred by major tech companies
  • Accredited by Malaysian Ministry of Education - Established since 1996
