University of Nottingham Malaysia (Nottingham)

A campus of the prestigious British university, main campus ranked #98 globally (in the UK)

Overview and Opportunities

The University of Nottingham Malaysia is an international campus of the renowned British university. It offers students the opportunity to obtain a prestigious British degree at a significantly lower tuition and living cost in Malaysia.

Opportunity to Transfer to the UK
  • (2+2) Engineering Pathway: Study for two years in Malaysia, then two years at the main campus in the UK or the China campus.
  • Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence: Possibility to transfer to the UK for the final year only.

Admission Requirements and Visa

Requirement Details
Application Fee 424 RM
English Language (Foundation) IELTS Score of 6.0
English Language (Bachelor's) IELTS Score of 6.5
EMGS Visa (Foundation) 3,150 RM (One-year visa)
EMGS Visa (Bachelor's) 3,900 RM (3-year visa)
Refund Policy

The EMGS visa fee is non-refundable. However, the visa deposit (2,000 RM) can be refunded in case of withdrawal and unused Visa Approval Letter (VAL), upon submitting a formal request.

Frequently Asked Questions

What scholarships are available for students at the University of Nottingham Malaysia?
The university offers the Provost's Excellence Scholarship with a 100% discount, the Dean's Excellence Scholarship with a 25% discount, the Sports and Arts Scholarship with a 50% discount, and a 10% Sibling Scholarship.
Is it possible to transfer to study in the UK?
Yes, you can transfer to the UK through the (2+2) engineering pathway, or transfer in the final year for Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence majors.
What are the English language requirements for admission?
The foundation program requires an IELTS score of 6.0, while Bachelor's and postgraduate programs require an IELTS score of 6.5.
