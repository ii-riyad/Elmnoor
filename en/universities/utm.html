Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM)

The leading public university in engineering and technology, ranked #153 globally (QS)

University Overview

Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM) is a public research university that is the top destination for studying engineering and technology in Malaysia. The university hosts around 5,468 international students out of a total of 19,467 students.

University Campuses
  • Johor Bahru (Main): Covering 1,222 hectares and offering most Bachelor's degree programs.
  • Kuala Lumpur (KL): Covering 38 hectares. Dedicated to Diploma programs, the Malaysia-Japan International Institute of Technology, and the Business School. Artificial Intelligence major is only available here.

Admission Requirements and Registration Dates

Requirement Details
English Language Pass Level 108 at the ELS Institute affiliated with the university.
Bridging The university does not accept the bridging system for Arab certificates. A Foundation or Diploma route must be completed.
Special Requirements for Certain Majors - Engineering (Electrical, Mechatronics, etc.): Pass the CBT exam.
- Architecture and Industrial Design: Personal interview and/or Portfolio.

Registration Dates

  • Phase 1: April 1 - May 15
  • Phase 2: June 23 - July 17
  • Foundation Program: Applications close on April 30

Accommodation and Living

On-campus accommodation provides a suitable environment at excellent prices:

  • Johor Campus Accommodation: Between 395 and 760 RM per month.
  • Kuala Lumpur Campus Accommodation: Between 385 and 935 RM for a single room, and up to 1,800 RM for a two-bedroom apartment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Bachelor's degree cost at UTM?
The cost of a Bachelor's degree is approximately 4,000 USD per year.
When does registration open at UTM?
Phase 1 applications open from April 1 to May 15. Phase 2 from June 23 to July 17. Meanwhile, Foundation program applications close on April 30.
How much is the first payment for UTM?
The first payment for a Bachelor's degree is approximately 18,608 RM, which includes application fees, visa, registration, refundable deposit, and the first semester installment. For the Foundation program, it is about 13,100 RM.
