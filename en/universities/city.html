City University Malaysia

Asian Ranking #558 | Affordable study options and exceptional support for Arab students

City University is one of the preferred destinations for Arab students in Malaysia due to the diversity of its majors, moderate fees, and continuous support for international students through special scholarships and English language courses to prepare students before joining the academic program.

Costs and Fees

Major / Item Cost
Bachelor's Annual $3,400 - $5,600
Application Fee 1,000 RM (~$250)
English Language Course ~2,000 RM per level
Late Payment Fines:

City University applies a fine of 100 RM per month on any overdue tuition fee balance. Therefore, students are advised to adhere to the specified payment deadlines.

Sudanese Students Scholarship

Exclusive Scholarship:

City University offers a special scholarship for students of Sudanese nationality worth 4,000 RM, deducted in the first academic year to support them.

Intake Dates

The university provides registration opportunities throughout the year across the following seasons:

  • January
  • May
  • September
  • July: Exclusively for the nursing major.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there English language courses available at the university?
Yes, if you do not have an IELTS certificate, the university provides English language courses to prepare you before starting your major at a cost of approximately 2,000 RM per level.
Can I study nursing in September?
No, the nursing major at City University has a dedicated intake which is July of each year.
