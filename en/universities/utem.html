Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM)

Malaysia's first public technical university with exceptional employment rates

University Overview

UTeM is Malaysia's first public technical university, located in the historical state of Melaka, a UNESCO World Heritage site. It distinguishes itself with a strong focus on practical and industrial training, making its graduates highly sought after in the job market.

Employment Opportunities

The university boasts a very high employment rate of 97%. Notably, about 30% of students receive actual job offers during their 6-month industrial training period.

Costs and Fees

UTeM fees are considered among the best and most economical in Malaysia for technical programs:

  • Total Cost for the Full Program: Ranges between 41,000 and 49,000 Malaysian Ringgit (RM) for the entire study period (not annually).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the total cost of studying at UTeM?
The total cost for the full program is approximately 41,000 to 49,000 Malaysian Ringgit.
What is the employment rate for UTeM graduates?
The university enjoys an employment rate of up to 97%, with 30% of students receiving job offers during their six-month industrial training period.
