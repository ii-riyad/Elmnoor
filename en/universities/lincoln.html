Lincoln University College

QS World Ranking #638 | Your preferred choice for studying Medicine and Health Sciences in Malaysia

Lincoln University is one of the leading private universities in Malaysia that focuses on medical and health science specialties. The university is distinguished by its competitive prices and offering a Doctor of Medicine (MD) program at affordable costs for Arab students.

Costs and Fees

Major / Item Cost (Estimated) Notes
Bachelor's (General Majors) $3,000 - $5,000 annually Varies by major
Human Medicine (MD) $15,000 - $17,200 annually Years 3-5 (Clinical) at Kota Bharu campus
EMGS Visa 3,500 RM Paid once in the first year
Very Important Fine:

The student must pay the full first-year fees before arriving in Malaysia. In case of non-payment, the university imposes a fine of 500 RM upon arrival.

Studying Medicine and Nursing

Lincoln University is distinguished by its Doctor of Medicine (MD) program, but there are some conditions and information to be aware of:

Alert for International Students in Medical Majors:

According to Malaysian laws, international students are not eligible for the House Officer period in Malaysian government hospitals. You must plan to complete the internship in your home country.

Nursing Requirements: Admission to the nursing major requires an IELTS certificate exclusively (minimum 6.0), and there are no exceptions or alternatives to this condition at Lincoln University.

Registration Dates and Location

  • Registration Dates (Intakes): March, June, September, November.
  • Geographical Location: The university is located in the Petaling Jaya area near Kuala Lumpur. Housing costs here are slightly higher due to its proximity to the capital, with living costs ranging from $400 to $700 per month.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there financial fines at Lincoln?
Yes, the university imposes a fine of 500 RM if the student does not pay the full first-year fees before arriving in Malaysia.
Where are the clinical years of medicine taught?
Clinical years (from the third to the fifth year) are taught at the Kota Bharu campus, which is specially equipped for medical training.
