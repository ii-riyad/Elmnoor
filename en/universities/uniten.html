Universiti Tenaga Nasional (UNITEN)

Leading Government-Linked University | 95% employment rate and guaranteed professional future

Universiti Tenaga Nasional (UNITEN) is a government-linked university owned by the national electricity company (Tenaga Nasional). It stands out for the strength of its engineering, computer science, and business programs, and the high employment rate of its graduates thanks to its close connection with the industry sector.

Exceptional Employment Rate

One of the most important reasons students choose UNITEN is the strength of its degree in the job market:

  • Graduate employability rate reaches 95%.
  • Strong internship programs that allow students to build a network with companies before graduation.

University Campuses and Majors

Two Campuses:
  • Putrajaya Campus: The main campus. Houses Engineering (all branches), Computer Science, and Information Technology (IT) majors. It is characterized by being located in the safe "green corridor", with hospitals and shopping malls very close by.
  • KSHAS Campus (Bandar Muadzam Shah — Pahang): Dedicated to business majors and includes: Accounting, Finance, Marketing, and Human Resource Management only.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is UNITEN classified as a public or private university?
It is a government-linked university directly owned by the national electricity company (GLC - Government Linked Company).
Where can I study electrical engineering at UNITEN?
All engineering majors, including electrical engineering, are studied at the main campus in Putrajaya.
