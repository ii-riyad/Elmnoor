Your guide to the accredited Arab high school certificates for admission into Malaysian universities

Accredited Arab Countries

Government high school certificates accepted in Malaysia

Accepted Certificates
  • Kingdom of Saudi Arabia
  • United Arab Emirates
  • Qatar
  • Jordan
  • Egypt
  • Libya
  • Sudan — Passing score of 50 or above
  • Yemen
UTM University — Does not accept Bridging programs

UTM does not accept bridging programs for Arab certificates. You must take the standard academic pathway (Foundation or Diploma, then direct Bachelor's depending on the qualification).

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Malaysian universities accept the Sudanese high school certificate?

Yes, Malaysian universities accept the Sudanese high school certificate, provided a minimum passing score of 50.

Does UTM accept Bridging programs for Arab certificates?

No, UTM does not accept bridging programs for Arab certificates. Students must go through the standard academic pathway (Foundation or Diploma, then Bachelor's).
