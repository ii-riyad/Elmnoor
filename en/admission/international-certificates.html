International Certificates for University Admission

Your guide to the accredited international certificates for admission into Malaysian universities and their requirements

Accepted International Certificates

Admission requirements and systems for various international certificates

British Certificate (IGCSE)
  • For Foundation Year:
    5 subjects with a minimum grade of C
  • For Direct Medicine:
    5 subjects with a grade of A or B, exclusively including:
    • Biology
    • Chemistry
    • Physics
    • Mathematics
    • English
American Diploma
  • The school must be accredited by:
    Cognia or NCA CASI
  • Some universities may require the SAT exam (evaluated out of 1600)
  • Preferred scores: 800 for Reading & Writing + 800 for Mathematics
International Baccalaureate (IB Diploma)
  • Must complete CAS requirements (Creativity, Activity, Service)
  • Must write the Extended Essay
  • The full diploma is required, not just individual subjects

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the requirements for accepting the British IGCSE certificate for Medicine?

For direct admission to Medicine, you must obtain 5 subjects with a grade of A or B, exclusively including: Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics, and English.

Is the American Diploma accepted without the SAT exam?

Some universities may request the SAT exam and consider it mandatory (evaluated out of 1600). The school must also be accredited by Cognia or NCA CASI.
