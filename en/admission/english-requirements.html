Frequently Asked Questions

Can I study English in Malaysia before my major?

Yes, most universities like UTM and City University offer English institutes and allow you to study it before starting your Bachelor's program.

What is the minimum English requirement for Nursing at Lincoln University?

The Nursing program at Lincoln University requires a minimum IELTS score of 6.0 and accepts no exceptions.
